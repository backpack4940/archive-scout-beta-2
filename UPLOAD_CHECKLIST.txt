20. Test included/excluded extensions, required/excluded keywords, regex, exact, case-sensitive, whole-word, and multiple keyword sets.
21. Test project backup, restore, merge, repair, diagnostics, reports, notes, tags, and review statuses.
22. Configure and verify Windows signing before creating a public release tag.
